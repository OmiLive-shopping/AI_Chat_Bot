🟢 Overview

Omi Live is a next-generation live shopping marketplace built for eco-conscious, independent brands. The platform enables small businesses to sell through interactive, story-driven livestreams, fostering community while driving sales.

🟣 Siplit - Our Lifestyle Brand

Siplit is the first lifestyle brand from Omi Live. It was created to encourage eco-friendly living in a fun and relatable way, especially for Gen Z. Siplit empowers socially awkward young adults to connect through conversation-starting, reusable aluminum water bottles.

Siplit is:

Women-owned and BIPOC-founded
Focused on self-expression with humorous hot-take designs like “I don't love work”, “My cat is cuter than yours”, and “Tennis > Pickleball”
Supporting reforestation by donating one tree per bottle sold
Running seasonal BOGO (Buy One Get One) promotions
Developing an upcoming AI text-to-design tool for personalizing bottles
Siplit helps Omi Live expand into youthful, socially conscious markets while staying true to its eco-first mission.

🟡 Mission and Core Belief

Omi Live believes that “1% Greener Matters.” That’s why:

Only eco-friendly brands that meet Omi’s sustainability standards are featured.
1% of all platform proceeds go to environmental and carbon-conscious causes.
We champion a more sustainable, transparent, and human way to shop — one where green brands thrive through community-powered live commerce.
Our motto: “If it's not green, it's not Omi.”

🟠 How Omi Live Works

For Brands:

List their products
Host live, interactive video shows
Use sustainability filters, storytelling tools, and collaborate with creators
For Shoppers:

Discover sustainable products during live shows
Engage in real-time Q&A and product demos
Shop instantly during livestreams, with full transparency
