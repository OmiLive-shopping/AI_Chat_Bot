Greenwashing: What It Is & Why We Don’t Tolerate It
Greenwashing is when brands make vague or misleading environmental claims to appear eco-friendly without real actions to back them up. Examples:

Using buzzwords like “natural” or “eco” with no proof
Highlighting one green initiative while hiding harmful practices
Fake certifications or unverifiable “carbon neutral” claims
Greenwashing erodes trust. It tricks people into thinking they’re doing good when they’re not.
At Omi Live, we fight greenwashing by demanding transparency: real sourcing, real certifications, real data. We help shoppers ask better questions, because informed customers push brands to improve.
